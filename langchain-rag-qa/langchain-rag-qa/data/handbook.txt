Company Handbook — Northwind Traders

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Requests must be
submitted to a manager at least 48 hours in advance. Core collaboration
hours are 10 AM to 3 PM in the employee's local time zone.

Paid Time Off
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off per year, plus 10
paid holidays. PTO accrues monthly and unused days roll over up to a
maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.

Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under $75 can be submitted with a receipt via the
expense portal and are typically reimbursed within 5 business days.
Expenses over $75 require manager approval before purchase.

Equipment Policy
New employees receive a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home-office
stipend during their first 30 days. Equipment requests after that go
through the IT helpdesk ticketing system.

Parental Leave
Employees are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave after 90 days
of employment. Leave can be taken continuously or split within the first
12 months after a child's birth or adoption.
